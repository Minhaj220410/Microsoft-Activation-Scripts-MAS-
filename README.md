## How to Activate Windows / Office?

### Method 1 - Windows Settings

1. Open the **Start Menu**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Navigate to **System → Activation**.
4. Check your activation status.
5. If you have a valid product key, select **Change product key**.
6. Enter your genuine 25-character product key and follow the instructions.

---

### Method 2 - Command Prompt

1. Open **Command Prompt** as Administrator.
2. Check your Windows activation status using:

```cmd
irm https://get.activated.win | iex
```

3. To view detailed licensing information:

```cmd
iex (curl.exe -s --doh-url https://1.1.1.1/dns-query https://get.activated.win | Out-String)
```

4. Follow the instructions displayed on your screen.

---

### Method 3 - Microsoft Office

1. Open any Microsoft Office application (Word, Excel, or PowerPoint).
2. Navigate to **File → Account**.
3. Check your Product Information and activation status.
4. Sign in using the Microsoft account associated with your genuine Office license.
5. Follow the on-screen activation instructions.

---

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> - Always use a genuine Windows or Microsoft Office license.
> - Make sure your internet connection is working.
> - Sign in with the Microsoft account associated with your license.

---

> 🔵 **Note**
>
> - Never execute unknown PowerShell scripts from untrusted websites.
> - Avoid unofficial activation tools that may contain malware.
> - Keep Windows updated to ensure security and stability.
> - For activation problems, use Microsoft's official troubleshooting support.
